RAINY CLOCK 1.8.0 (38)

iPhone-only, iOS 17+. Alarms use AlarmKit on iOS 26+ and local notifications on iOS 17-25.

NEW: HOME SCREEN AND LOCK SCREEN WIDGET
Earlier notes said the app had no Home Screen widget; it now has one, "Tomorrow's Alarm". It needs iOS 26+, as it shares the AlarmKit Live Activity extension. iPad compatibility mode offers no widgets, so please test on an iPhone.

Sizes: Home Screen small and medium; Lock Screen rectangular, circular and inline. It shows tomorrow's expected alarm time and why: earlier because rain is forecast on the route, or skipped for a holiday, a non-repeat day or a date set to silent. The medium size adds home and work weather with the Apple Weather mark. It matches the app's Tomorrow card.

The widget makes no network requests and shows no ads. It reads a snapshot (no addresses) the app writes to its App Group whenever it runs, including background refresh.

To test:
1. Open the app; set Home and Work in Settings > Route and the time in Settings > Time; allow alarms. Until the app has run, the widget says "Open the app to show tomorrow's alarm".
2. Home Screen: long-press an empty area > Edit > Add Widget > search "Rainy Clock" (Chinese: "雨天鬧鐘") > small or medium > Add Widget.
3. Lock Screen: long-press > Customize > Lock Screen > tap the widget area > Rainy Clock.
4. Change the time in Settings > Time; the widget follows.

No login, Sign in with Apple or demo account is needed; Apple-verified app and purchase data recognize membership.

PURCHASES
Settings > Other > Membership:
- Monthly, auto-renewable: com.shukaihu.RainyClock.plus.monthly
- One-time, non-consumable: com.shukaihu.RainyClock.banner.lifetime
Both remove banner ads, unlock calendar features and include one daily AI generation without an ad (shared if both are owned). No annual plan. A one-time purchase does not cancel a subscription. The top-right menu has Restore purchases (benefits only, not alarms or audio), Manage subscriptions and Delete membership data (does not cancel Apple subscriptions).

CALENDAR AND ALARM TEST
Paid plan: Settings > Calendar > enable calendar rules, choose Taiwan office or US federal holidays (with observed dates). In the alarm calendar, tap a date to toggle ring/silent and confirm at top right; orange dots mark overrides.

Settings > Route: type Home and Work (e.g. Taipei Main Station, Taipei 101), pick a suggestion, choose a travel mode. In Time, set the wake-up time, rain threshold and lead time. Enable the test weekday, set a near-future alarm, lock the iPhone. The Alarm page shows tomorrow; check today's test at its time. Failed updates never clear scheduled alarms.

A Taiwan closure feature in the binary is disabled in this version (no entry point, no push registration).

TYPHOON CLOSURES (TAIWAN)
Paid plan: Settings > Calendar > Use temporary closure rules (work, school or both; districts). A matching official closure (DGPA via NCDR) skips that day's alarm and shows on the widget. Push registers only while on; without valid data, alarms ring.
[END OPTIONAL]

ADS AND TRACKING
One LevelPlay banner; rewarded ads for extra AI generations. On first launch the Apple tracking prompt appears; in the EEA, UK and Switzerland the app's own consent sheet comes first and the prompt follows when it closes. The ad SDK stays off until they are answered; declining changes nothing else. If none appears, check Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. Report an ad and (consent regions) Ad privacy options: Settings > Other > Privacy & ads; "Report this ad" is under the banner.

AI AND SANDBOX
Settings > Time > a sound > AI Voice: enter a line, pick a voice; previews work offline. Free: one initial generation; paid: one per day. Extras need a completed rewarded ad; saved audio uses no quota. Our backend and Google speech services process the text (see privacy policy). In Sandbox/TestFlight, membership data are isolated and production ads are off, so rewarded ads may be unavailable.
