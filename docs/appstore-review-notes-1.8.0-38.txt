RAINY CLOCK 1.8.0 (38)

iPhone-only, iOS 17+. Alarms use AlarmKit on iOS 26+, local notifications on iOS 17-25. No login or demo account; Apple-verified purchase data recognize membership.

NEW: "Next Alarm" WIDGET (iOS 26+)
Small, medium; Lock Screen rectangular, circular, inline. It shows the next alarm (today's from midnight until it rings, then tomorrow's) and why (e.g. rain, a holiday or a closure). Only medium shows weather, with the Apple Weather mark; tapping it opens Apple's legal attribution page via the app. No network or ads; it reads an address-free snapshot from the app.
Test on an iPhone (iPad compatibility mode has no widgets). After Route and Time setup, add Rainy Clock from the Home Screen (long-press > Edit > Add Widget) or Lock Screen (long-press > Customize). Before the app first runs it reads "Open the app to show your next alarm".

NEW: TYPHOON CLOSURES (TAIWAN)
Settings > Calendar > "Use temporary closure rules": off by default; both paid plans, not free. A verified official closure (DGPA via NCDR) for the home or work district skips that day's alarm; failed or unverified data never changes it. With no typhoon there is normally none; tap "View map demo" below the switch (every plan, no purchase): marked "Demo data · Not live", fictional, changes nothing.
Turning it on asks once for notification permission; push registration follows the rule, and turning it off deletes it. Pushes carry no location; a push alone never cancels an alarm. Addresses are geocoded with Apple Maps on the device to find districts; no location permission. The phone sends our service a random installation ID, the APNs token and a device-held credential (the server keeps only its SHA-256 hash), plus a receipt after each check (revision, times, applied/no-alarm). Addresses, districts, routes and alarm times are never sent to it.

NEW: ALARM SWITCH (every plan)
The switch beside the Alarm title offers "Turn Off Next Alarm Only" or "Turn Off Until I Turn It Back On"; the card then shows "Skipped" or "Alarm Off". The card shows today's alarm, headed "Next alarm", until its time passes, then tomorrow's ("Tomorrow").

TRACKING PERMISSION (2.1)
On first launch after install, Apple's tracking request appears before the ad SDK starts; the SDK stays uninitialized until the choice is known. iOS shows it only if Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking > Allow Apps to Request to Track is on. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland the app's own ad-privacy choice comes first; the request follows once it closes. Declining limits no feature.

LOCATION TEST (2.1(a))
Settings > Route > Home: type Taipei Main Station, pick the suggestion. Work: type Taipei 101, pick the Xinyi Rd. Sec. 5 result. The route preview appears. A typed name also works unpicked.

PURCHASES
Settings > Other > Membership: monthly auto-renewable com.shukaihu.RainyClock.plus.monthly; one-time non-consumable com.shukaihu.RainyClock.banner.lifetime. Both remove banner ads and include calendar and closure rules and one daily AI generation without an ad. One-time does not cancel a subscription. Top-right menu: Restore purchases, Manage subscriptions, Delete membership data.

CALENDAR AND ALARM
Paid plan: Settings > Calendar > "Use calendar rules", then tap dates in "Alarm calendar" to toggle ring/silent. Ring test: Settings > Time, wake-up time a few minutes ahead; Settings > Calendar, include today in "Repeat days"; allow alarms; lock the iPhone. Failed updates never clear alarms.

AI VOICE, SANDBOX
Settings > Time > a sound > "AI Voice". Free: one initial generation; paid: one daily; extras need a completed rewarded ad. Our backend and Google speech services process the text. Sandbox/TestFlight membership data are isolated; production ads are off, so ads may not load.
