RAINY CLOCK 1.8.1 (41)

iPhone-only, iOS 17+. Alarms use AlarmKit on iOS 26+, local notifications on iOS 17-25. No login or demo account; Apple-verified purchase data recognize membership.

CHANGES SINCE 1.8.0 (40)
Maintenance release: no new feature, permission, endpoint or data. Membership: a device whose App Attest key our server rejects now registers a new key (still attested, replay-checked) instead of failing every launch; the membership screen's diagnostic line names the server's error code. Lock Screen rectangular widget: the English header no longer truncates.

"Next Alarm" WIDGET (iOS 26+)
Small, medium; Lock Screen rectangular, circular, inline. Shows the next alarm (today's until it rings, then tomorrow's) and why (rain, a holiday, a closure). Small (forecast background) and medium (weather column) show the Apple Weather mark. Tapping that column opens Apple's legal page via the app; small opens the app's Alarm tab, whose weather card links to it. No network or ads; it reads an address-free snapshot from the app.
Test on an iPhone; iPad compatibility mode has no widgets. After Route and Time setup, add Rainy Clock from the Home Screen (long-press > Edit > Add Widget) or Lock Screen (long-press > Customize).

TYPHOON CLOSURES (TAIWAN)
Settings > Calendar > "Use temporary closure rules": off by default; both paid plans, not free. A verified official closure (DGPA via NCDR) for the home or work district skips that day's alarm; failed or unverified data never changes it. No typhoon, no announcement; "View map demo" below the switch (every plan, no purchase) is fictional, marked "Demo data · Not live", and changes nothing.
Turning it on asks once for notification permission and registers for pushes; turning it off deletes that. Pushes carry no location; a push alone never cancels an alarm. Addresses are geocoded with Apple Maps on the device to find districts; no location permission. The phone sends our service a random installation ID, the APNs token and a device-held credential (the server keeps only its SHA-256 hash), plus a receipt after each check. Addresses, districts, routes and alarm times are never sent to it.

ALARM SWITCH (every plan)
The switch beside the Alarm title offers "Turn Off Next Alarm Only" or "Turn Off Until I Turn It Back On"; the card then shows "Skipped" or "Alarm Off".

TRACKING PERMISSION (2.1)
On first launch, Apple's tracking request appears before the ad SDK starts, which stays uninitialized until the choice is known. iOS shows it only if Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking allows requests. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland the app's own ad-privacy choice comes first; the request follows once it closes. Declining limits no feature.

LOCATION TEST (2.1(a))
Settings > Route > Home: type Taipei Main Station, pick the suggestion. Work: type Taipei 101, pick the Xinyi Rd. Sec. 5 result; the route preview appears.

PURCHASES
Settings > Other > Membership: monthly auto-renewable com.shukaihu.RainyClock.plus.monthly; one-time non-consumable com.shukaihu.RainyClock.banner.lifetime. Both remove banner ads and include calendar and closure rules and a daily AI generation. One-time does not cancel a subscription. Top-right menu: Restore purchases, Manage subscriptions, Delete membership data.

CALENDAR AND ALARM
Paid plan: Settings > Calendar > "Use calendar rules", then tap dates in "Alarm calendar" to toggle ring/silent. Ring test: Settings > Time, wake-up time a few minutes ahead; Settings > Calendar, include today in "Repeat days"; allow alarms; lock the iPhone.

AI VOICE, SANDBOX
Settings > Time > a sound > "AI Voice". Free: one initial generation; paid: one daily; extras need a rewarded ad. Our backend and Google speech services process the text. Sandbox/TestFlight membership data are isolated; production ads are off, so ads may not load.
